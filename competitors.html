What This Tells You
They spend on customers. Nobody spends on staff.

CNP Cleaning has Google Ads conversion tags running — money going into winning customers. None of the four has a single page aimed at the operative they need to do the work.

So every firm competes for staff on the job boards, with the same kind of advert, for the same people. That is why a listing on its own struggles: it is one of a couple of hundred.

The firm that does it properly — UK Window Clean

A national window cleaning operator based in Leeds. It has a dedicated recruitment page with a video about the company, and two rules on it worth noticing:

  • Applications only through its own online form — no emails, no calls. Everyone answers the same questions.
  • Everyone goes through its training regime, even experienced candidates, working first alongside a team leader.

That is the filter your audit describes: make people answer your questions before they reach you, and set the standard up front so the wrong ones do not apply.

Where You Can Win
The lane is empty in London
  • No London competitor we checked is recruiting through its own site, so a proper MSCS recruitment page with qualifying questions stands out immediately.
  • You have the story — over twenty years, London and Kent contracts, hot-water and pure-water kit, SMAS accreditation, and branded kit in your own photos.
  • You already have analytics installed, so a recruitment page can be measured from day one.
  • Your own photos show real MSCS operatives in branded hi-vis on real jobs — exactly what an experienced cleaner wants to see before moving.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Whether these firms advertise vacancies elsewhere — Indeed, other job boards, social media, agencies. We checked their own websites only.
  • Purity Window Cleaning could not be checked reliably on the day and is left out.
  • Anyone's pay rates, hiring numbers or staff turnover. We have seen public websites only.
  • Every cleaning firm in London. This is the set we could find and check today.

Everyone is on the same job boards. Nobody is building their own front door.

That is the opening.
