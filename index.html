Recruitment Audit · MSCS Cleaning Ltd · London & Kent

640 Interviews. 2 Hires. 1 Still There.

Prepared for Mario Petralia · MSCS Cleaning Ltd · 14 September 2026 · AG1 Consulting
640:1
interviews for every person who stayed

You do not have a shortage of applicants. You have no filter between the advert and the interview.

Indeed did its job. It sent you applicants — hundreds of them. The problem is that every bit of the sorting happened in your interview room, one person at a time.

If each interview took just twenty minutes, that is over 200 hours of your time to find two people, one of whom has already gone. That is not a hiring process. That is a second job.

Your figures, as you gave them to our team · site read 14 September 2026
Finding 1 — The Filter Is In The Wrong Place
How it works today: Indeed advert -> anyone can apply -> you interview them -> you find out What it produced: Interviews ............ 640 Hired ................. 2 Still with you ........ 1 How it should work: Advert -> they answer your questions first -> only the qualified reach you -> interview
Job boards are built for volume

A job board's job is to get you as many applicants as possible, from people who are often applying to a lot of jobs at once. That is useful when anyone can do the role. It is the wrong tool when you need someone who already knows how to use a water-fed pole, turns up, and represents your name on a client's site.

The fix is not a better advert on the same board. It is asking the qualifying questions before anyone gets an interview, so the 638 who were never right rule themselves out.

Finding 2 — Your Website Says Nothing To An Applicant
Pages on mariossupercleaningservices.co.uk: 14 Pages about working for MSCS: 0 What your homepage tells a customer: "fully qualified and insured operatives" "site managers regularly inspect the premises" "Thermopure Hot Water system" What it tells a good operative deciding where to work: nothing
Good operatives check you out before they apply

The people you actually want are mostly already working for somebody else. Before they move, they look you up. Right now they find a site written for customers, with no page about the work, the kit, the areas, the standards or why MSCS is a better seat than where they are.

You have a genuinely good story — over twenty years, commercial and residential contracts across London and Kent, proper equipment, SMAS accreditation. None of it is aimed at the person you are trying to hire.

Finding 3 — You Are One Advert Among Hundreds
The job boards are crowded with exactly the same role

Glassdoor listed 206 window cleaner jobs in London in July 2026 — and that is one job board. Your Indeed advert sits on the same shelf as all of them, competing on the one thing a listing shows — a job title and a rate.

The people who are good at this are not scrolling that shelf. Reaching operatives who already do the work, around London and Kent, whether or not they are job hunting, is how you stop competing with two hundred other adverts for the same few applicants.

Finding 4 — "Qualified" Is Not Written Down Anywhere
The questions that decide who reaches an interview: Water-fed pole / pure water experience .......... yes / no / how long Roof, gutter, pressure washing, carpet work ..... which ones Full UK driving licence ......................... yes / no Right to work in the UK ......................... yes / no Areas they can reach (London / Kent) ............ which When they can start ............................. date
If you cannot write it down, you cannot filter on it

At the moment "qualified" lives in your head, so the only way to test it is to interview. Written down as six questions, it becomes a filter that runs while you are on a job — and anyone who cannot answer never takes up a slot in your diary.

The same page is also where the real pay, hours and what the work involves go. People who would leave after a week rule themselves out before you have trained them.

Two Small Fixes While You Are Here
Unrelated to hiring, and worth ten minutes
  • One contact block on your homepage shows the wrong mobile number. It reads and dials 079 5725 3219, while everywhere else on the site says 07957 153219. One digit, and those callers never reach you.
  • Your SMAS accreditation only appears as a logo image. A picture cannot be read by Google or by somebody skimming on a phone. Put it in words too.

The Method, In Four Steps
  • Go where qualified people already are. Put the job in front of people who already do this work, around London and Kent, not only the ones browsing job boards.
  • Make them qualify themselves. Six questions before an interview. Can't answer? No slot in your diary.
  • Stop the early leavers. Real pay, hours and what the job involves, up front.
  • Measure the right thing. Cost per person hired, and whether they are still with you after 90 days — not how many applied.

Fewer applicants. The right ones. Before they reach your diary.

Indeed gives you people. This gives you people who have already answered your questions.
